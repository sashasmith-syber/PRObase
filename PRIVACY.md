# Privacy Notice

AIT PROfessor processes your prompts and attachments to generate responses.

## Data Handling

- **Local storage only**: conversation history is stored in your browser.
- **No analytics by default**: the app does not include tracking scripts.
- **No PII logging**: sensitive data is not intentionally logged.

## Tips

- Avoid sharing sensitive or personal information in prompts.
- Clear chat history from the UI when finished.
